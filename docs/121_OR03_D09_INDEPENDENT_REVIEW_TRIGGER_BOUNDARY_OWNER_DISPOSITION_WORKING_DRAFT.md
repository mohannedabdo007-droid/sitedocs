## 1. Decision question
How should the requirement for independent review be triggered and controlled without conflating discipline-conflict resolution with independent assurance or inventing project risk thresholds?

## 2. Owner disposition
**Conditionally approved.** The owner accepts the following principles:

1. Mandatory independent review is required only where an approved, versioned project risk-and-authority matrix (or an expressly applicable approved governance instrument) defines the trigger and the authority competent to determine that the trigger is met.
2. The matrix must identify applicable trigger criteria, decision authority, scope of application and the required independent-review control. This disposition does not create or approve any such matrix, trigger class, risk threshold or authority hierarchy.
3. A disagreement between disciplines, by itself, does not automatically create an independent-review requirement. Conflict classification, technical conflict resolution/escalation and independent review are separate functions. A conflict decision or technical-lead concurrence cannot waive independent review where the approved matrix requires it.
4. Any independent reviewer must have evidenced competence, formal appointment and independence for the required subject and scope. The review scope, applicable response version and supporting evidence must be identifiable and attributable.
5. Review findings and any clearance are tied to the exact response version and evidence actually reviewed. A material change to the response, relevant contribution, assumptions, scope, technical conclusion or material evidence requires documented impact assessment and reassessment of whether the approved matrix requires targeted or full re-review. Prior clearance is not silently carried forward to a changed version.
6. If no approved/versioned matrix exists, or its applicability or trigger determination is ambiguous, no substitute criterion or threshold is to be invented. The affected independent-review determination remains held and is referred to a competent, evidenced project authority for decision.

## 3. Conditions and unresolved prerequisites
- Specialist validation is required from the project/process owner, appointing/project-governance authority, technical/risk authority, QA/independent assurance and relevant discipline authorities as applicable.
- The actual approved/versioned risk-and-authority matrix and its approval, ownership, applicability and decision-authority evidence must be identified before operational use.
- Reviewer competence, appointment, independence, conflict-of-interest assessment and exact review scope must be evidenced for each applicable review.
- Re-review materiality and scope must be reconciled with OR03-D10 and the detailed DD-04 specification; this disposition does not settle all change-impact criteria.
- Any missing, expired, disputed or ambiguous matrix/authority/reviewer evidence leaves the affected review decision held; no implied fallback authority is permitted.

## 4. Explicit exclusions
This disposition does not approve or create a risk matrix, define universal trigger classes or thresholds, automatically require review for every discipline disagreement, appoint reviewers, grant capabilities or permissions, waive any required independent review, establish final reviewer-substitution rules, or authorize workflow, UI, API, code, database, Storage, authentication, authorization/RLS, test execution, production-data operation, backup/restore, merge or deployment.
