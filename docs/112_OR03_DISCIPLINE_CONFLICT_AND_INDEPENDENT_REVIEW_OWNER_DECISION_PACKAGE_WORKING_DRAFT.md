## 1. Purpose and decision rules

OR-03 covers discipline responsibility, technical leadership, conflict and dissent governance, and the boundary with independent review (DD-03/DD-04). This package prevents one broad approval from accidentally appointing people, approving a risk matrix, granting capabilities or authorizing implementation.

Permitted disposition for each item:
- **Approve** — accept the stated principle within a precisely recorded scope.
- **Approve with conditions** — accept only the explicit principle and conditions recorded.
- **Amend and return** — revise the candidate and present it again.
- **Reject** — reject the candidate principle with rationale.
- **Defer** — no decision until named prerequisite/evidence is available.

A specialist review is validation/advice unless an explicit valid appointment grants decision authority for that subject and scope. Silence, attendance, informal agreement and a review without authority evidence are not approval.

## 3. Detailed decision sheets

### OR03-D01 — Discipline scope and responsibility

**Decision question:** Should the required discipline set be determined from the actual engineering question, project scope, interfaces and approved discipline taxonomy by an evidenced technical authority?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve this principle; exact appointing authority and scope evidence remain specialist prerequisites.
- B. Amend the decision authority or required evidence before approval.
- C. Defer pending the project's approved discipline taxonomy and authority instrument.

Guardrail: no discipline is added or removed solely by subject keywords, staff availability, historical participation or coordinator preference.

### OR03-D02 — Discipline contribution accountability

**Decision question:** Should each required discipline own a separately attributable contribution, with submission, administrative completeness, technical assessment and technical confirmation treated as distinct conditions?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve the separation as the baseline.
- B. Approve only after the contribution record fields and confirmation authority are revised.
- C. Defer pending discipline-specific validation.

Guardrail: an uploaded file, assignment acceptance or coordinator status update is not technical confirmation. A required contribution cannot be made optional by convenience.

### OR03-D03 — Technical lead mandate

**Decision question:** Should the technical lead coordinate interfaces, evaluate completeness, prepare consolidated candidates and resolve only matters inside explicit competence and delegation?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve bounded coordination/consolidation mandate; reserve scope, appointment, readiness and issue decisions to separately evidenced authorities.
- B. Amend the list of technical decisions the lead may make.
- C. Defer pending appointment/delegation model review.

Guardrail: no appointment is created; no authority is inferred from job title.

### OR03-D04 — Conflict classification and blocking

**Decision question:** Should C0–C4 remain candidate descriptors while materiality, severity, blocking scope and objective examples are validated by competent technical/risk authorities?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve the classification concept only, with no final thresholds.
- B. Return C0–C4 for specialist redesign.
- C. Defer until the project risk/technical authority supplies approved criteria.

Guardrail: the labels are not severity levels and cannot themselves encode risk ranking. Unresolved material technical disagreement blocks the affected response content.

### OR03-D05 — Conflict decision authority and escalation

**Decision question:** Should the technical lead resolve only within explicit delegation, with out-of-scope or disputed matters held for the appointed higher technical authority?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve the bounded escalation principle.
- B. Amend the escalation route and reserved matters.
- C. Defer pending project governance/appointment evidence.

Guardrail: escalation does not transfer authority automatically; the coordinator cannot act as technical tie-break.

### OR03-D06 — Dissent and qualified positions

**Decision question:** Should every material dissent and qualified technical position remain visible, attributable and linked to its evidence even when an authorized decision selects another direction?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve preservation and reasoned disposition of dissent.
- B. Amend what must be shown in the issued response versus retained in the controlled evidence record.
- C. Defer pending records and discipline review.

Guardrail: no majority vote, silence-as-concurrence or silent overwrite.

### OR03-D07 — Discipline interfaces and checklists

**Decision question:** Should discipline-specific checklists be validated by the relevant discipline authorities rather than being declared mandatory by this generic framework?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve illustrative prompts only; require discipline validation before operational use.
- B. Amend the discipline taxonomy or interface coverage.
- C. Defer until appointed discipline authorities review.

Guardrail: ARC/CIV/MEC/ELE/SURVE labels and prompts are not substitutes for the project's approved discipline taxonomy, engineering standards or design criteria.

### OR03-D08 — Separation of functions

**Decision question:** Should contribution, discipline validation, technical leadership, conflict decision, independent review, readiness and formal issue remain distinct decision functions?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve distinct functions; permit combinations only when project governance expressly allows them and conflict/self-review controls are satisfied.
- B. Require stricter separation for specified functions after QA/security review.
- C. Defer pending project governance and risk matrix.

Guardrail: this does not create a blanket two-person rule or dual-signature requirement. Any combination must be evidenced and checked against applicable independence rules.

### OR03-D09 — Independent-review trigger boundary

**Decision question:** Should mandatory independent review be triggered only by an approved, versioned risk/authority matrix with a named owner and applicability authority?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve the matrix-governed trigger principle; no trigger classes or thresholds are approved here.
- B. Amend the required matrix ownership and approval route.
- C. Defer until the actual risk matrix and authority instrument are produced.

Guardrail: a conflict resolution or technical lead's concurrence cannot waive a mandatory independent-review requirement.

### OR03-D10 — Re-review after changes

**Decision question:** Should changes to the response candidate or source contribution/evidence require impact assessment against the exact reviewed version, followed by targeted or full re-review where the approved matrix requires it?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve exact-version impact assessment and matrix-governed re-review.
- B. Amend materiality and impact-assessment criteria.
- C. Defer pending DD-04 and QA validation.

Guardrail: review clearance never transfers automatically to a materially changed candidate.

### OR03-D11 — Conflict record and history

**Decision question:** Should conflict records preserve original positions, evidence, classification, authority, rationale, dissent and successor versions, with closure of the conflict separate from parent RFI closure?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve the evidence/history separation principle.
- B. Amend record ownership, correction or retention controls.
- C. Defer pending records, architecture and security review.

Guardrail: no deletion, silent merge or rewriting of predecessor technical positions is authorized.

### OR03-D12 — External authority and contractual boundary

**Decision question:** Should owner/client/designer/engineer-of-record/external-authority concurrence be required only when an approved project or contract governance instrument expressly says so?

Candidate paths:
- A. Approve the evidence-bound rule.
