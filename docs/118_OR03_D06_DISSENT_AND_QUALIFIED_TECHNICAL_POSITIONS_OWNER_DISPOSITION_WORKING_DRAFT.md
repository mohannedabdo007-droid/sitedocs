## 1. Owner disposition

The owner conditionally approves the principle that every material dissenting, qualified or otherwise non-concurring technical position must remain attributable, accurately represented and traceable throughout the RFI decision record. A decision that does not adopt a position does not establish that the position was wrong, withdrawn or accepted by its author.

The record must preserve, at minimum:
- the original submitted position in its source form, including its author, discipline, submission time, exact version and linked evidence;
- any qualification, reservation, limitation, assumption, condition or explicit non-concurrence;
- subsequent clarification or revision as a separately attributable successor record, without silently replacing the original;
- the classification of the issue, affected response content/interface and any stated technical or project impact;
- the disposition of the position and the decision-maker's identity, authority, scope, rationale and evidence;
- the author's explicit concurrence, if later given; concurrence must never be inferred from silence, participation, administrative completion or failure to object.

## 2. Required disposition of each dissent or qualification

Each material dissent or qualified position must receive an explicit recorded treatment, using a reasoned outcome appropriate to the case, such as:
- **Adopted:** the position is accepted within a stated scope;
- **Partially adopted:** identified elements are accepted and the remaining elements are separately addressed;
- **Not adopted:** the authorized decision-maker records why the position was not selected, with the technical basis and evidence considered;
- **Unresolved / referred:** no final disposition is claimed; the affected content or decision remains subject to the applicable hold and escalation rules.

These are record-treatment descriptors, not new parent RFI lifecycle states, approval codes or automatic severity rankings. A not-adopted position remains part of the historical record and must not be represented as its author's concurrence.

## 3. No erasure, rewriting or false concurrence

No technical lead, coordinator, reviewer or decision-maker may:
- delete or silently overwrite the original dissent or qualified position;
- rewrite its meaning to make it appear compatible with the adopted response;
- attribute concurrence to its author without explicit attributable confirmation;
- treat a majority vote, silence, non-response or administrative status as technical agreement;
- omit a material qualification from the controlled decision history merely because another authority selected a different position.

Corrections to a record must be attributable and traceable, preserving the predecessor content and reason for correction. Later clarification or a changed view must be recorded as a new version/event linked to the original, not as a retroactive alteration.

## 4. Decision record versus formal response presentation

The controlled RFI decision/evidence record must preserve the complete attributable positions and their dispositions, subject to applicable access controls. The formal response should present material unresolved or qualified matters, the adopted technical position and any limitations necessary to avoid a misleading representation. It need not reproduce every internal working note verbatim, but it must not conceal a material unresolved issue or imply unanimous agreement where none exists.

The exact external presentation, confidentiality, privilege and records rules remain subject to project governance, contract and specialist review; this disposition creates no new disclosure or legal rule.

## 5. Relationship to conflict blocking and issue

The existence of dissent alone does not automatically block the entire RFI or prevent issue where the disagreement has been validly resolved by an evidenced competent authority within its mandate. However:
- a material technical conflict that remains unresolved continues to block the affected response content under OR03-D04;
- a dispute about decision authority remains held under OR03-D05;
- a recorded decision does not waive mandatory independent review, technical requirements, safety/regulatory controls or other applicable issue gates;
- the scope released after resolution is limited to the content and dependencies actually addressed by the authorized decision.

No contributor is deemed to have concurred merely because an authorized decision was issued.

## 6. Conditions and specialist validation

This disposition remains conditional on specialist validation by affected discipline authorities, the project/process owner, appointed technical/conflict authority, Document Control/records, QA/independent assurance, security/privacy and contract/project governance as applicable.

Specialists must validate:
- the minimum fields and source evidence needed to preserve dissent and qualified positions;
- correction, clarification, successor-version and audit-history handling;
- how material dissent is summarized in formal responses without distorting source meaning;
- access, confidentiality, privilege and retention boundaries under approved project instruments;
- consistency with OR03-D01–D05, DEC-103/108/109, DD-04 independent review and the approved project records framework.

No record schema, workflow, permission, appointment, retention period or implementation behavior is approved here.
