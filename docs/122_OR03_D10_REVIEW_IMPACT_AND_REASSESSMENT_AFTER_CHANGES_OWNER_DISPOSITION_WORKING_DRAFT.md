## 1. Decision question
How should a change to a response candidate or its supporting contribution/evidence be assessed so that previous review clearance is not misapplied, while re-review remains proportionate to the affected scope?

## 2. Owner disposition
**Conditionally approved.** The owner accepts the following principles:

1. A change that may affect the exact response version reviewed, its assumptions, scope, a discipline contribution, technical conclusion, material evidence, interface or dependency requires a documented impact assessment against the exact reviewed package/version.
2. The competent technical authority assesses the materiality and affected scope of the change only within evidenced current appointment, competence, project scope and explicit delegation. The authority and rationale for that assessment must be attributable.
3. Where re-review is required, it may be targeted to affected portions where dependencies and interfaces permit; full re-review is required where the evidenced impact or applicable approved governance instrument requires it. A limited change does not automatically restart the entire RFI, and a broader dependency must not be ignored.
4. Independent re-review is mandatory only where required by the approved/versioned risk-and-authority matrix under OR03-D09. This disposition does not create a new independent-review trigger or waive an existing one.
5. Previous review comments, decisions and clearance remain historical and apply only to the exact version and evidence actually reviewed. They do not transfer silently to a successor or changed response version. Any new clearance must identify the exact version and scope reviewed.
6. Preserve the reviewed version, change description, impact rationale, affected scope, decision authority, review decision, reviewer evidence and any re-review outcome. Corrections and successor versions must not overwrite the predecessor review record.
7. Where materiality, dependencies, applicable matrix requirements or decision authority are missing, disputed or ambiguous, hold the affected clearance and refer the matter to a competent, evidenced authority. Do not invent numerical or categorical thresholds.

## 3. Conditions and unresolved prerequisites
- Specialist validation is required from the project/process owner, technical and discipline authorities, QA/independent assurance, appointing/project-governance authority, architecture, security and records as applicable.
- The applicable approved/versioned risk-and-authority matrix under OR03-D09 must be identified before deciding whether independent re-review is mandatory.
- Project-specific change-control, technical materiality criteria, interface/dependency rules and delegated decision authority remain to be validated; this disposition does not define universal thresholds.
- Exact-version identity, immutable review evidence and traceable successor relationships must be reconciled with DD-04 and the evidence/version-control specifications.
- Targeted versus full re-review criteria require specialist review; where uncertain, hold the affected clearance pending competent authority determination.

## 4. Explicit exclusions
This disposition does not define universal materiality thresholds, automatically require full RFI restart, authorize carry-forward of old clearance, appoint technical authorities or reviewers, approve a risk matrix, create capabilities or permissions, alter lifecycle states, or authorize workflow, UI, API, code, database, Storage, authentication, authorization/RLS, test execution, production-data operation, backup/restore, merge or deployment.
