## 2. Conditionally approved principle

1. Determination of the disciplines required for an RFI belongs to the currently authorized technical authority for the relevant project, party and scope.
2. The determination must be reasoned from the engineering question, project scope, technical interfaces and relevant discipline responsibilities.
3. The RFI coordinator performs administrative recording, routing and follow-up only. The coordinator does not independently decide which disciplines are technically required, remove a required discipline, or resolve a technical scope dispute.
4. Discipline scope and any resulting assignment must be supported by current, attributable evidence of the appointing/technical authority, delegated scope and applicable project governance.
5. No discipline responsibility or authority may be inferred from job title, membership, keyword matching, availability, prior participation or administrative convenience.

## 3. Conditions and outstanding validation

This disposition is conditional. It does not identify or appoint an actual technical authority, discipline authority, technical lead or coordinator, and does not grant capabilities.

Required specialist validation remains pending, including:

The exact project discipline taxonomy, authority instrument, evidence fields, appointment term/revocation, dispute escalation and change-control details remain open until validated.

## 4. Interim control

Where technical authority, scope, discipline responsibility or appointment evidence is missing, expired, disputed or ambiguous, hold the affected assignment/transition and refer it through the approved project governance route. The coordinator must not fill the gap by inference.
