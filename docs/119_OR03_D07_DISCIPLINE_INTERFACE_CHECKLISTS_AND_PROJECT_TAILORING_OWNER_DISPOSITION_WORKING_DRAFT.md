## 1. Decision question
How should discipline-interface prompts and technical checklists be governed across ARC, CIV, MEC, ELE and SURVE without treating illustrative design material as universally mandatory project requirements?

## 2. Owner disposition
**Conditionally approved.** The owner accepts the following principles:

1. Discipline checklist prompts for ARC, CIV, MEC, ELE and SURVE are illustrative design aids at this stage. They are not approved technical checklists and are not universally mandatory requirements.
2. A checklist, or any part of it, may become mandatory for a project or discipline only after review and approval by the competent, properly appointed authority for that discipline, with authority, competence, scope and applicable delegation evidenced.
3. Approval must be contextual: the checklist must be assessed against the engineering question, project scope, applicable codes and specifications, discipline interfaces, project governance instruments and required evidence.
4. Checklists should help identify, as applicable, cross-discipline interfaces, dependencies, required inputs and outputs, responsible contributors, evidence expectations, assumptions and unresolved matters.
5. Project-specific additions, tailoring, exclusions or deviations must be attributable, justified, version-controlled and traceable to the applicable project and approval authority.
6. Omission of a prompt or checklist item does not by itself constitute a waiver, acceptance, technical resolution or removal of an obligation arising from an applicable code, specification, contract or approved project instrument.
7. No generic checklist is to be imposed on every project without contextual review and approval. Discipline-specific technical requirements remain with the competent authority and applicable project instruments.

## 3. Conditions and unresolved prerequisites
- Specialist validation is required from the relevant discipline authorities and applicable project/process, QA, architecture, security and records reviewers.
- Actual appointment, competence, delegation and project-scope evidence must be verified before any checklist is approved for operational use.
- Applicable codes, specifications, contractual/project instruments and evidence requirements must be identified for the project concerned.
- Interface ownership and treatment of cross-discipline dependencies must remain consistent with OR03-D01–D06 and the DD-03/DD-04 review framework.
- Any unresolved authority, scope, applicability or technical requirement remains open and must not be resolved by administrative assumption.

## 4. Explicit exclusions
This disposition does not approve or issue actual ARC/CIV/MEC/ELE/SURVE checklists; establish universal mandatory checklist content; appoint any discipline authority, technical lead or contributor; create capabilities or permissions; waive technical, contractual, regulatory or independent-review requirements; define a project-specific technical standard; or authorize workflow, UI, API, code, database, Storage, authentication, authorization/RLS, test execution, production-data operation, backup/restore, merge or deployment.
