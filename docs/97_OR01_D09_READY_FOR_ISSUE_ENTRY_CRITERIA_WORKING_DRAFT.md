## 1. Decision question and scope

DISP-OR01-009 defines the evidence and authority needed before a response candidate may enter the proposed parent state **Ready for Issue**. It must answer:

1. Which technical, contribution, conflict, clarification, independent-review, scope, evidence and version gates must be complete?
2. Who confirms readiness, and what is the relationship between that confirmation and the separately appointed technical issuer?
3. Is a separate attributable readiness confirmation required, and what evidence must it contain?
4. What changes invalidate readiness or require a fresh readiness assessment?
5. Which checks must be repeated at the moment of formal issue?
6. How is readiness kept separate from formal issue, technical closure, Transmittal dispatch, delivery, access and acknowledgement?

The candidate lifecycle in docs/72 distinguishes **Ready for Issue** (a pre-issue state) from **Closed** (after authorized formal issue and technical closure). This memorandum refines the former only. Formal issue and technical closure remain subject to DISP-OR01-010.

## 2. Protected governance and prior owner dispositions

1. **DEC-104/105:** the coordinator is administrative; technical authority and technical lead act only within evidenced appointment/delegation; the technical issuer must be specifically authorized. Job title, project membership, reviewer status or dispatch activity alone grants no issue authority.
2. **DISP-OR01-004:** technical authority determines disciplines, scope, required lead and assignments; coordinator only checks administrative readiness and executes expressly delegated routing. No role or assignment is inferred.
3. **DISP-OR01-005:** open valid parent-progression clarification blockers prevent progression; partial responses/receipt alone do not resolve them; technical sufficiency is confirmed by an authorized technical actor.
4. **DISP-OR01-006:** every required contribution must be complete and technically confirmed or formally removed from required scope by authorized evidenced scope change; provisional parallel preparation is not formal completion.
5. **DISP-OR01-007:** material rework returns affected work under its separate route; preserve contributions/candidates and create attributable successor versions.
6. **DISP-OR01-008:** where mandatory independent review applies, the approved matrix, reviewer appointment/independence and exact-version review must be valid; blocking comments prevent Ready for Issue; clearance never transfers automatically to changed content.
7. **DEC-106:** requested dates do not create default SLA, clock pause or contractual consequence; formal issue and closure remain governed separately.
8. **DEC-107:** RFI ID, formal revision, response version and evidence IDs remain distinct; submitted/issued evidence and version history must be traceable.
9. **DEC-108:** the authorized technical authority issues the formal response; Document Control distributes it through controlled Transmittal for formal external distribution. Distribution/acknowledgement are not technical approval.
10. **DEC-110:** acceptance evidence must trace requirement, decision, exact version, authority, outcome and supporting evidence. No tests are executed by this memorandum.

## 3. Proposed readiness model

### 3.1 Meaning of Ready for Issue

Ready for Issue is a **pre-issue technical readiness determination** for one exact response candidate/version. It means the defined readiness criteria have been evidenced and the candidate may be presented to the separately appointed technical issuer for a final issue-time authorization check.

It does not mean:
- the response has been formally issued;
- the RFI is Closed;
- the response has been distributed or delivered;
- recipients have accessed or acknowledged it;
- an external contractual notice or deemed receipt has occurred;
- Document Control, the coordinator or independent reviewer has acquired issue authority.

The candidate must remain visibly not-issued until the formal issue event succeeds.

### 3.2 Recommended readiness checklist

The following checklist is recommended as a mandatory evidence gate, subject to specialist validation and owner disposition.

**A. Authority, scope and assignment integrity**
- Current RFI identity, formal revision/cycle and project/party context are unambiguous.
- Technical authority, technical lead, required discipline scope and assignments have valid appointment/delegation evidence.
- Every required discipline assignment is complete and technically confirmed, or has been formally removed from required scope through an authorized, evidenced scope change.
- Optional inputs are explicitly classified by competent authority; no required input is downgraded by convenience, silence or workload.
- Current scope, assignment and authority evidence are not stale, expired, revoked or disputed.

**B. Required contribution completeness**
- Each required contribution has a stable identity, accountable discipline/author, exact version, technical completion confirmation and required evidence references.
- Contribution completeness is confirmed by the authorized discipline authority or other actor designated by the approved model; a coordinator's administrative check is not technical acceptance.
- Any provisional consolidation/preparatory artifact is excluded from evidence of completed required contribution.
- Late, changed, superseded or invalidated contributions have been reconciled and the candidate uses the correct current versions.

**C. Clarifications, assumptions and technical conflicts**
- No valid parent-progression clarification blocker remains open.
- Each clarification response has been technically assessed for sufficiency by an authorized technical actor; receipt or partial response alone is insufficient.
- Material technical conflicts, cross-discipline dependencies, assumptions, deviations and dissent are explicitly dispositioned by the competent authority with reasons and evidence.
- No unresolved material conflict is converted to agreement by silence, majority vote, elapsed time or administrative closure.
- Any change requiring formal revision or linked new RFI has followed DEC-022-A/B and DEC-106 rather than being absorbed silently into the candidate.

**D. Independent review**
- The approved, applicable risk/authority matrix and its version/trigger assessment are recorded.
- If review is mandatory, reviewer appointment, competence, scope, independence, conflict declaration and access are valid for the exact candidate/package.
- Review outcome is a valid passing outcome for the exact frozen response version and manifest; all blocking comments are resolved and closure verified under the approved rule.
- If review is not mandatory, the authorized trigger determination and matrix/version rationale are recorded. Absence of a review record is not evidence that review was not required.
- Any conditional/limited clearance is usable only if expressly permitted by the approved matrix, all conditions are objectively closed before readiness, and the evidence is retained.
- No review clearance is inherited by a changed candidate without the impact assessment and re-review required by DISP-OR01-008.

**E. Exact response package and evidence integrity**
- The response candidate is complete, coherent, attributable and versioned.
- A frozen readiness package manifest identifies the exact response version, contribution versions, attachments, supporting records, controlled references, assumptions, conflict dispositions and applicable review evidence.
- Every included evidence item is accessible to the authorized decision-makers and its identity/version is unambiguous.
- No mutable live document, unversioned attachment or unexplained reference is relied upon as the approved source.
- The readiness determination is linked to the exact candidate and package manifest; changes create successor versions, not silent replacement.

**F. Readiness decision and authority**
- An authorized readiness function records that each applicable criterion is satisfied, identifies the evidence, notes exceptions/conditions, and confirms the exact candidate/version and manifest.
- The readiness record identifies the decision-maker, appointment/delegation basis, date/time, scope, rationale and any dissent or limitation.
- Any exception is permitted by an approved rule, expressly authorized, bounded and evidenced. No exception may waive mandatory technical, safety, regulatory, independent-review or other binding requirements.
- Readiness confirmation is not formal issue authorization and does not allow the confirmer to issue unless separately appointed to do so.

## 4. Separate readiness confirmation — recommendation

Recommend a distinct, attributable **Readiness Confirmation Record** bound to the exact response candidate/version and package manifest. It should capture:

1. RFI ID, formal revision/cycle, candidate ID and response version;
2. package manifest identifier/version and included source contribution/evidence versions;
3. current project, party, discipline scope and assignment snapshot;
4. checklist result for each readiness domain in §3.2, with evidence references;
5. clarification blocker status and technical sufficiency decisions;
6. conflict/dissent/deviation/assumption dispositions and competent authority;
7. independent-review trigger assessment and exact-version outcome, or authorized no-review determination;
8. readiness confirmer identity, appointment/delegation and authority scope;
9. limitations, approved exceptions, dissent and outstanding non-blocking observations;
10. decision timestamp, parent lifecycle/version precondition and immutable history link.

This is a recommendation for an explicit record, not a requirement for two different people or dual signatures. Whether the readiness confirmer and issuer may be the same individual depends on the approved appointment/separation model. No role combination is inferred here. The issuer must independently possess valid issue authority and must re-check all issue-time guards.

## 5. Transition into Ready for Issue

Proposed entry should be authorized only after all applicable §3.2 gates pass:

- From **Under Consolidation**, the appointed technical lead submits the exact candidate with a complete readiness package and valid trigger assessment.
- From **Under Independent Review**, a valid passing outcome for the unchanged exact version is recorded; no blocking comment remains.
- Where independent review is not required, the authorized matrix determination and rationale are recorded; the no-review path is not inferred from absence of reviewer activity.
- The readiness confirmer or expressly authorized readiness function records the checklist result and exact-version confirmation.
- The parent transition is revalidated against current state, authority, scope, assignments, blockers, contributions, review outcome, candidate version and evidence manifest at action time.
- If any required criterion is missing, ambiguous, stale, disputed or failed, the candidate remains in its prior governed state or is routed to the relevant separate rework/clarification path; it must not be marked Ready for Issue.

No coordinator-only click, UI state, elapsed time, absence of comments, notification or dashboard calculation may create readiness.

## 6. Changes after readiness and invalidation

Readiness is version- and evidence-bound. The following require the readiness record to be reassessed and normally a new successor candidate/readiness confirmation:

- response content or technical conclusion changes;
- required contribution, scope, assignment or discipline changes;
- new/changed clarification response or blocker;
- new material conflict, dissent, assumption, deviation or technical dependency;
- attachment, drawing, calculation, specification or controlled-reference version changes;
- independent-review outcome, comment disposition, reviewer appointment or matrix applicability changes;
- authority appointment, delegation, access or project/party scope expires, is revoked or changes;
- evidence integrity, package manifest or source lineage is found incomplete or stale.

A purely clerical/metadata change may be classified as non-impacting only by an authorized actor under a separately approved rule, with a recorded impact assessment demonstrating that technical content, scope, evidence and package identity are unchanged. It must not mutate the prior readiness record or frozen package.

A material change after readiness requires the candidate to return to the appropriate prior stage under DISP-OR01-007/008/005 as applicable. It must not be handled by editing the Ready for Issue record in place.

## 7. Final issue-time gate

Ready for Issue does not reserve or guarantee future issue. Immediately before formal issue, the specifically appointed technical issuer must re-check:

1. issue authority and delegation are current and cover the project, party, discipline, response and action;
2. parent remains Ready for Issue and no withdrawal, cancellation, reopening, supersession or other conflicting transition has occurred;
3. candidate and package manifest exactly match the readiness confirmation;
4. all required contributions, clarification blockers, conflict dispositions and mandatory review outcomes remain valid and unchanged;
5. no new technical, scope, evidence, appointment, access or matrix change invalidates readiness;
6. required issue metadata and formal revision/response identifiers are correct and distinct;
7. issue record and closure evidence can be committed without partial state or duplicate issue.

If any check fails or is uncertain, issue must be held and the candidate routed for reassessment. The issuer cannot rely solely on a previously displayed Ready for Issue badge or stale approval record.

## 8. Formal issue, closure and Transmittal boundaries

- **Readiness:** a pre-issue determination that gates the candidate for issuer consideration.
- **Formal issue:** a separate authorized technical act that makes the exact response version the official issued response under the approved process.
- **Technical closure:** remains governed by DEC-106 and DISP-OR01-010; the candidate docs/72 model prefers successful formal issue and associated closure to be committed together. This memorandum does not finally approve that transaction design.
- **External distribution:** formal external distribution of the issued response is controlled through Transmittal under DEC-108; Document Control distributes but does not issue.
- **Internal access:** any authorized internal availability is separate from formal external dispatch.
- **Delivery/access/acknowledgement/substantive response:** separate recipient-level events under DEC-103/108; none creates or reverses readiness or technical issue.
- **Post-issue change:** use authorized reopening/new-cycle, clarification, formal revision or linked new RFI routes. Never silently edit the issued response or its readiness/issue evidence.

## 9. Failure, concurrency and stale-state safeguards

- Re-check current parent state, candidate/version, package manifest, assignments, scope, blockers, review outcome, matrix, authority and appointments at readiness transition and issue action time.
- Concurrent candidate edits, scope changes, new blockers, review comments, withdrawal/reopening actions or competing issue attempts must prevent stale readiness from advancing.
- Readiness confirmation and issue must be idempotent and attributable; retries must not create duplicate confirmations, formal issues or closure events.
- Failed issue attempts must not create a partially issued response or falsely close the RFI. Preserve attempt/failure evidence without changing the official issued record.
- A stale readiness record must be rejected/held, not silently refreshed against a different candidate.
- Detailed atomic transaction, idempotency and recovery design requires architecture/QA/security validation; no implementation mechanism is selected.

## 10. Security, access and records

All readiness evidence and package attachments must be visible only to actors authorized for the exact project/party/discipline/appointment context. Preview/download and issue actions require current server-side authorization. Readiness access does not grant editing, issue, external distribution or broader project visibility. Preserve the decision, package, evidence, authority and event lineage according to approved records rules. Retention, legal hold and disposition periods remain unresolved under the separate records workstream.

10. Confirm that exceptions require an approved rule and cannot waive mandatory technical or governance controls.

## 13. Recommended interim controls

Until owner disposition and specialist validation are complete:

- Do not treat Ready for Issue as an issued response or a closed RFI.
- Do not infer readiness from UI action, coordinator action, elapsed time, no comments or dashboard status.
- Do not bypass incomplete required contributions, open blockers, unresolved material conflicts or mandatory independent review.
- Do not infer a readiness confirmer or technical issuer from title or membership.
- Do not allow a changed/stale candidate or evidence manifest to inherit readiness.
- Do not treat readiness as authority to distribute; formal external distribution remains controlled by Transmittal.
- Hold any uncertain authority, scope, contribution, review, package or version evidence.

## 15. Technical recommendation summary

Adopt a controlled Ready for Issue gate with: (1) complete and current authority/scope/assignment evidence; (2) every required contribution complete or validly removed from scope; (3) no open parent blocker or unresolved material conflict; (4) required independent review validly passed for the exact frozen version, or an authorized matrix-based no-review determination; (5) a complete immutable response/evidence package; (6) a distinct attributable readiness confirmation recommended, but not presumed to require dual signatures; (7) separate, current issue authority and final action-time revalidation; (8) readiness kept separate from formal issue, technical closure, Transmittal, delivery and acknowledgement.
