## 1. Decision question
How should the RFI operating model distinguish internal technical assessment and Document Control communication from external client, designer, consultant, engineer-of-record, regulator or other authority involvement, and how should it avoid inventing contractual or legal effects?

## 2. Owner disposition
**Conditionally approved.** The owner accepts the following principles:

1. External concurrence, approval, consent, no-objection, instruction or other formal involvement is required only where an applicable contract, approved project governance instrument, statutory requirement or other competent governing instrument expressly requires it. The applicable instrument and clause/requirement must be identified and validated; no generic external approval gate is created.
2. No external authority, decision right, appointment, mandate or contractual status may be inferred merely from an organization's identity, job title, seniority, distribution-list membership, system access, participation, comment submission or recipient/acknowledgement status. The relevant role, mandate, scope and authority must be evidenced by the applicable governing instrument and/or formal appointment.
3. An internal RFI technical assessment, conflict disposition, technical confirmation, readiness confirmation or formal response does not by itself replace, imply or satisfy statutory approval, permit, regulator consent, client instruction, engineer-of-record approval, contract variation, formal notice or any other external requirement.
4. External comments, reviews, approvals, consents, no-objections or instructions must be recorded with their source and identity, evidenced authority and scope, applicable governing instrument/reference, exact document/response version, date/time, conditions, limitations and supporting evidence. Classify the involvement as advisory, review, consent, approval, no-objection or formal instruction only where that classification is defined by the applicable instrument; do not assign legal or contractual effect by label alone.
5. Document Control distribution, technical delivery receipt, access, receipt acknowledgement or other communication event is not substantive technical acceptance, owner approval, regulatory approval, contractual consent or formal instruction unless the applicable governing instrument expressly and validly defines that effect.
6. If external authority, the applicable contractual route, notice requirements, required recipient, timing, form, conditions or legal/contractual effect is missing, disputed or ambiguous, hold the affected decision or action and refer it to the competent project, contract, legal or regulatory authority. Do not invent deadlines, deemed receipt, deemed consent, silence-as-approval or waiver.
7. Specialist validation by contract/commercial, legal, project governance, Document Control/records, technical, regulatory and security/privacy specialists is a separate prerequisite as applicable. This disposition creates no legal opinion, contractual interpretation or statutory determination.

## 3. Conditions and unresolved prerequisites
- Identify the actual project contract, approved governance instrument, statutory/regulatory requirement or formal appointment that governs each external role and required action.
- Confirm the competent authority, mandate, scope, validity period, delegation, recipient identity and any applicable notice or response requirements from the actual instrument.
- Reconcile external review/approval records with the exact RFI response version, Transmittal/package identity and recipient-level delivery/acknowledgement semantics under DEC-103 and DEC-108.
- Keep external technical comments and decisions attributable and version-bound; preserve conditions, limitations, dissent and superseded records under OR03-D06 and OR03-D11.
- Confirm privacy, records, retention and disclosure controls under applicable approved instruments; no retention or disclosure rule is created here.
- Obtain exact-version specialist findings and evidence. No specialist approval is claimed.

## 4. Explicit exclusions
This disposition does not interpret any actual contract, create a legal opinion, determine statutory obligations, appoint a client/designer/consultant/regulator or other authority, grant external decision rights, establish notice periods or deemed effects, define a universal external approval matrix, change DEC-103/108 recipient aggregation, create a workflow or form, or authorize UI/API/code, DB, Storage, authentication, authorization/RLS, test execution, production-data operations, backup/restore, merge or deployment.

## 5. Owner confirmation
**Owner decision:** Conditionally approved.  
**Exact scope:** External concurrence only where expressly required by an applicable validated governing instrument; no inferred authority; internal RFI assessment does not replace external/statutory/contractual approval or instruction; external involvement must be attributable and tied to authority, scope, instrument and exact version; distribution/delivery/access/acknowledgement do not imply substantive acceptance; ambiguity is held and referred without invented deadlines or deemed consent; specialist legal/contract/project/technical validation remains mandatory as applicable.  
**Conditions:** Actual governing instruments, appointments, authority, notice requirements and specialist findings remain prerequisites.
