## 1. Owner disposition

The owner conditionally approves OR03-D02: each required discipline must have a separately attributable contribution, linked to the responsible assigned contributor and to the exact contribution version and supporting evidence. The contribution record must distinguish the following conditions rather than treating them as interchangeable:

1. **Submitted** — a contribution has been submitted and its submission is recorded.
2. **Administratively Complete** — the required administrative fields and evidence references are present and pass the applicable administrative completeness check. This is not a technical conclusion.
3. **Technically Assessed** — the contribution has undergone a technical assessment by an appropriately authorized and competent reviewer; the assessment outcome and rationale are recorded. Assessment alone is not necessarily confirmation.
4. **Technically Confirmed** — the contribution has been explicitly confirmed by the authorized technical authority for the relevant discipline, scope and exact version, with attributable decision evidence and any conditions recorded.

The four conditions describe distinct evidence states. A later condition must not be inferred merely because an earlier event occurred. Upload, receipt, assignment acceptance, coordinator review, or administrative completeness does not establish technical assessment or technical confirmation.

## 2. Accountability and evidence conditions

For each required discipline contribution, the candidate record must be attributable to:
- the relevant parent RFI and response cycle;
- the required discipline and its authorized scope;
- the named responsible contributor and the evidenced assignment/appointment applicable to that contribution;
- the exact contribution version and linked supporting evidence;
- submission and administrative-completeness events;
- technical assessor identity, competence/authority evidence, assessment outcome and rationale, where assessed;
- technical confirmer identity, authority scope, decision, date/time, exact version and conditions, where confirmed;
- any return, correction, superseding version, qualification or dissent, preserving predecessor history.

A revised contribution is a distinct version. Prior assessment or confirmation must not silently transfer to a changed version. The applicable authority must determine whether the change requires reassessment or reconfirmation; no automatic carry-forward is presumed.

## 3. Authority and safeguards

- The contributor is accountable for the submitted contribution within the evidenced assignment.
- Administrative completeness is checked as an administrative control and cannot substitute for professional/technical judgment.
- Technical assessment and technical confirmation must be performed only by persons whose competence, appointment and scope are evidenced for the relevant action.
- The RFI coordinator may record, route and follow up the contribution administratively, but cannot convert submission or administrative completeness into technical confirmation.
- No authority is inferred from job title, project membership, availability, prior participation, or coordinator convenience.
- If contributor assignment, discipline scope, assessor/confirming authority, competence evidence or exact version is missing, expired, disputed or ambiguous, the affected technical gate remains on hold and is referred through the approved project governance route.
- A missing or incomplete required contribution cannot be silently treated as optional or as concurrence.

## 4. Conditions, exclusions and dependencies

This disposition is conditional on specialist validation by the affected discipline authorities, technical authority, project/process owner, Document Control/records, architecture and security/QA reviewers as applicable. The review must validate the contribution fields, assignment evidence, version lineage, technical assessment/confirmation authority, correction and supersession handling, and exact-version revalidation.

This disposition does not:
- appoint any actual contributor, assessor, discipline authority or technical confirmer;
- approve a final RACI, appointment instrument, capability catalogue or permission grant;

OR03-D02 is a conditional owner disposition only. Specialist validation remains pending; OR-03 remains open until the other decision items and required reviews are completed.
