## 1. Decision question
How should separation of duties and role boundaries be maintained across RFI contribution, technical leadership, conflict resolution, independent review, readiness, issue, coordination, distribution and recipient acknowledgement?

## 2. Owner disposition
**Conditionally approved.** The owner accepts the following principles:

1. The following functions are distinct and must not be treated as interchangeable merely because one person holds a project title or participates in the RFI: (a) preparing a technical contribution; (b) discipline technical assessment/confirmation; (c) technical-lead coordination and consolidation; (d) material conflict decision; (e) independent review; (f) readiness confirmation; (g) formal issue authorization; (h) RFI administrative coordination; (i) Document Control distribution; and (j) external recipient acknowledgement.
2. A person may perform only functions for which current appointment, competence, project scope and explicit delegation are evidenced. A role title, seniority, membership, availability, prior participation or administrative convenience does not itself confer another function's authority or capability.
3. A person must not independently review or approve their own work where separation is required by the applicable approved project/risk/authority matrix or governance instrument. The exact self-review prohibition and any permissible exception must be validated against that matrix; no universal matrix is invented here.
4. No default dual-signature or two-person approval requirement is created by this disposition. Additional signatures or independent approvals apply only where expressly required by an approved project authority, risk matrix, contract or governance instrument.
5. Actual, potential or perceived conflicts of interest must be disclosed and assessed by a competent authorized authority. That authority determines appropriate mitigation, restriction, reassignment or recusal, with the basis and outcome recorded. The affected person must not self-clear a material conflict where independent determination is required.
6. Role combination is not automatic. Where separation is impracticable, any exception requires a documented, case-specific justification, explicit approval by the competent project-governance authority, an assessment of the resulting risk, and defined compensating controls, scope and duration. The exception must be traceable and subject to review; silence or operational convenience is not approval.
7. Administrative coordination and Document Control distribution do not confer technical decision, readiness or issue authority. Recipient acknowledgement is a communication event and does not constitute technical acceptance or approval.

## 3. Conditions and unresolved prerequisites
- Specialist validation is required from the project/process owner, appointing/project-governance authority, relevant discipline and technical authorities, QA/independent assurance, security/architecture and Document Control as applicable.
- The approved project authority/risk matrix and any contract/governance rules governing self-review, incompatible duties, signatures and exceptions must be identified and reconciled before operational use.
- Actual appointments, competence, delegation, conflicts-of-interest controls and exception-approval authority must be evidenced; none is created by this owner disposition.
- Any role conflict, missing authority, unclear separation requirement or unapproved combination remains held for competent governance determination. No implied fallback authority is permitted.
- This disposition must remain consistent with OR03-D01–D07, DEC-105–DEC-110, DISP-OR01-008–010 and DD-04 independent-review requirements.

## 4. Explicit exclusions
This disposition does not appoint any person, establish a final RACI or authority hierarchy, create roles/capabilities/permissions, approve a risk matrix, grant self-review exceptions, impose a universal dual-signature rule, define contractual/legal effect, or authorize workflow, UI, API, code, database, Storage, authentication, authorization/RLS, test execution, production-data operation, backup/restore, merge or deployment.

## 5. Owner confirmation
**Owner decision:** Conditionally approved.  
**Exact scope:** Distinct RFI functions and action-level authority; no inferred capability from titles; self-review prohibited where the approved matrix requires separation; no default dual-signature rule; conflicts of interest disclosed and assessed by competent authority; role combinations only by documented, approved, risk-based exception with compensating controls.
